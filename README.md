# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things
My favorite series is Stranger Things. It is a science fiction, horror, and mystery series that follows a group of friends who experience strange events in their town. The story takes place in Hawkins. The characters discover a mysterious alternate dimension called the Upside Down.

### My favorite characters
- Dustin
- Steve
- Max

### Why I like this series
1. It has an interesting storyline.
2. It has exciting and mysterious scenes.
3. Every episode makes me curious about what happens next.

**Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery
